# Examples

## A good weekly briefing (week 2)

**Herons 0-1 after Mesa Verde loss; safety coverage ranks last**

We are 0-1 and 6th of 6. We lost 10-30 at home to Mesa Verde; Mateo Okafor (QB)
went 24 of 44 for 229 yards with 1 touchdown, and Emeka Hargrove (DL) had 2 sacks.

Around the league, Iron Falls beat Summit 24-23 on the road and Lakeshore beat
Delta Bay 26-7 on the road.

Our biggest weakness is safety coverage: 6th of 6, at 48.5 against a league
average of 71.8. Theo Strickland (coverage 52, $4.1M) and Nikola Kowalski
(coverage 45, $2.98M) start there.

We have $5.5M of cap space under the $255M hard cap, and the trade deadline is
after week 9, 7 weeks after this one. This week we're away at Iron Falls (1-0,
3rd of 6).

*Watching:* whether Iron Falls goes deep against our safeties.

Why it works: the result leads, every number is in the facts, one idea per
paragraph, and the weakness names the players.

## A bad one

**A massive week ahead as the Herons look to bounce back in a must-win clash!**

The Herons, who lost by 20 points last week, are now 0-1. Our safeties are
about 23 points below average, which is a huge problem. With roughly $5M in
space we have room for an elite upgrade, and it's worth noting the deadline is
coming up.

What's wrong with it:

- The headline is 14 words and all hype ("massive", "must-win").
- "Lost by 20" and "23 points below average" are numbers worked out by the
  writer, not in the facts. Give the score and both ratings instead.
- "Roughly $5M" rounds the $5.5M the facts give.
- "The Herons" in the third person; it's "we".
- "Elite upgrade" promises something the cap may not allow; "it's worth noting"
  is filler; "the deadline is coming up" leaves out the week.

## A good answer to a question

*Can we afford a $6M safety?*

**Answer:** Not as we stand: we have $5.52M of space, so a $6.0M safety puts us
$0.48M over the hard cap. Sending out any player earning $0.48M or more first
makes it fit.

**Recommendation:** Pair the signing with a trade of Nikola Kowalski ($2.98M),
which leaves $2.50M of room afterwards.

**Evidence:**
- Payroll $249.48M, cap space $5.52M (Cap & Contracts, from the league)
- $6.0M signing: $0.48M to clear (Cap & Contracts, check_cap_fit)
