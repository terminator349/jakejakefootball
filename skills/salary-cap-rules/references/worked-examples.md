# Worked examples

All numbers are from the Herons at the start of the season: payroll $249.48M,
cap space $5.52M, 48 players. The cap is $255M.

## Signing a free agent

A $5.0M safety: 5.52 - 5.0 = $0.52M left. It fits, but it's the last move we can
make without sending salary out: the cheapest contract in the league is $1.00M.

A $6.0M safety: 5.52 - 6.0 = -0.48. It doesn't fit; $0.48M must be cleared
first, by trading away at least that much salary.

## A one-for-one trade

We send Theo Strickland ($4.10M) and get a $9.87M safety back:
249.48 - 4.10 + 9.87 = $255.25M payroll, $0.25M over the cap. Illegal. To make it
legal we must send $0.25M more out, or take back a cheaper player. Roster size is
unchanged at 48.

## A two-for-one trade

We send Strickland ($4.10M) and Nikola Kowalski ($2.98M) for one $9.87M safety:
249.48 - 7.08 + 9.87 = $252.27M, $2.73M of space left. Roster 48 - 2 + 1 = 47, above
the 46 minimum. Legal for us. The other team takes in $7.08M and sends out
$9.87M, so its payroll falls by $2.79M, and its roster grows by 1: check it stays
at or under 53.

## The deadline

In week 9 a trade is still allowed. In week 10 every trade is refused, whatever
the money, until the season ends.

## What this league doesn't have

No dead money, no signing bonuses, no restructures, no cap exceptions, no
"room next year". If a question assumes one of these, say the league doesn't
have it and answer with the rules above.
